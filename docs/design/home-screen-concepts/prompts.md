```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: high-fidelity mobile home-screen design exploration for the existing g7m strength-training logger.
Create a polished landscape presentation showing TWO large flat front-facing mobile app screens side by side, dark theme on left, light theme on right, both the SAME design. Light colors: base #f3f5f0, white cards, dark text, deep green #3f6417 primary button with white text. Restrained 16px corner radii, precise alignment, generous tap targets, crisp thin icons.
Preserve greeting "Bok, Milan" and circular M avatar. Preserve the FOUR EXISTING month stat cards AS A HORIZONTAL SWIPE CAROUSEL: Personal records, Workouts, Muscle groups, Strength & activity. Show the first existing card large, titled "Personal records", "This month", "3 new bests", simple trophy icon and subtle mini bar chart, footer "Stronger than last month". Show narrow edge of next card on right and exactly FOUR pagination marks under rail. Do not replace with generic calorie/steps cards or a 2x2 stat grid. Screen should look like one normal carousel state, not four full cards stacked.
Refine Quick access to only "Calendar", "Browse exercises", "Progress", with calendar/search/chart line icons; large easy tap surfaces. Start action is promoted OUT of the equal quick-access tiles and becomes the unmistakable main task. Exact main CTA text "Start a workout", supporting text "Choose a routine or start blank". No invented AI recommendations or readiness scores.
Bottom navigation: Learn, Friends, Home, Profile, Settings, with Home active. Keep page content clear of tab bar. Optional small "Today's plan" row below quick access. Accessible contrast, no photos, no excessive decoration, no gradients, no tiny text. These are alternative design concepts, not implemented screenshots.
Option title outside screens: "01 / Workout first". Layout: greeting, existing single-card-tall month carousel, then a prominent full-width filled green action panel approximately 105px tall with large dumbbell icon left, "Start a workout" headline in bold 22px, subtitle "Choose a routine or start blank", and circular play affordance right. The entire panel is one start action, visually dominant and purposeful. Below it "Quick access" with THREE equal compact icon tiles in a single row, label Browse exercises wrapping neatly. Then a small quiet coach observation. Keep all main actions visible above tabs. Strong priority hierarchy and minimal clutter.
```
